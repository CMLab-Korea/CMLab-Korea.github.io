How to connect.

01

Study our research

Review recent publications and identify the problems you want to work on.

02

Prepare your materials

Introduce your background, concrete research interests, coding experience, and relevant work.

03

Contact early

Prospective graduate students are encouraged to contact the PI approximately 3–6 months before applying.

04

Discuss research fit

A paper discussion, coding task or undergraduate research experience may help assess mutual fit.
